# v1.23 - Spoiler-conscious overviews and local downloads

Local candidate: **1.23.0+24**. Not a published release.

- Replace plot excerpts on resume and title overviews with concise year, genre,
  director and cast facts. Full synopses require opening a labelled spoiler warning.
- Ask AI discovery for brief, spoiler-conscious recommendations; confirmed title
  cards use the same catalog facts as Home.
- Keep the animated Zanzibarr wordmark and logo in a fixed Home header, beside
  navigation controls. Narrow windows use a menu.
- Download original NZB files to a chosen local folder, independently of streaming
  archive parsing, with progress and cancellation. Open downloaded videos using
  the existing player and resume history, without an NNTP connection.

Archive sets require extraction with a separate local archive tool before opening
an extracted video. This does not fix the RAR4 streaming parser, repair missing
articles, or resume interrupted download jobs. Downloads are explicit, limited to
200 GiB per job, and must remain on the download screen until complete.

See [implementation and verification](../SPOILER_FREE_LOCAL_DOWNLOADS_2026-10-20.md).

Unsigned setup and portable ZIP are available locally under `dist/windows/v1.23`;
checksums and bundle contents passed verification. Not installed or published.
