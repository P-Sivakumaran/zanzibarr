# zanzibarr v1.17 — Film & TV discovery and keyboard controls

Local candidate: **1.17.0+18**. Not a published release.

- Ask Codex, Claude or Gemini for films, TV shows, miniseries or mixed lists.
  Cards label each title as Film or TV show; TV cards open TV release search.
- Keep films and shows in one playlist. Saved film-only playlists remain
  compatible. New AI replies must identify each title's type.
- Use Enter to send and Shift+Enter for a new line, plus Ctrl/Cmd+Enter,
  Escape to stop, new-chat/focus/tab/settings/help shortcuts and native editing.
  Windows/Linux redo also accepts Ctrl+Y in the composer. IME composition is
  allowed to complete without submitting a request.
- Open keyboard help from the toolbar, Ctrl/Cmd+/ or F1. New chat and stopped
  requests preserve the draft. Replies scroll to their beginning and keyboard
  focus returns to the composer when the chat remains active.
- Update discovery navigation, examples and playlist wording for films and TV.

These are ordered title lists. Automatic episode selection, queues and playback
are not added. AI recommendations do not verify indexer availability.

Full gate passed: 266 Rust and 337 Flutter tests (8 opt-in screenshot skips),
Clippy, analysis, host build and whitespace checks. Nineteen focused TV/chat
checks passed with preview generation. Windows release, setup and portable ZIP
built; SHA256 hashes, ZIP integrity and required components verified. See
[verification](../TV_DISCOVERY_KEYBOARD_2026-10-18.md).

Unsigned, not installed or published. No new live model request,
