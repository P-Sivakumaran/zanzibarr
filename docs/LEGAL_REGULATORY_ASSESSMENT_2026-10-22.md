## Software licensing and assets

Local checks found notices for selected dependencies, but no root `LICENSE` or README license declaration. Public GitHub visibility permits viewing/forking under GitHub's terms; it does not establish the general modification/distribution grant needed for external releases. Request provenance from upstream and check historical commits and any separate permission correspondence before deciding what license can apply. This check has not inspected private correspondence. [GitHub licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository).

mpv's own copyright file says its default is GPLv2+ and an LGPL build requires excluding GPL-only code; linked components can change the outcome. FFmpeg likewise distinguishes LGPL from GPL-enabled builds. Preserve exact library source, build flags, modifications and appropriate license texts; audit source distribution and user replacement/relinking requirements for the actual linking arrangement. Neither a wrapper MIT notice nor a public application repository alone proves these obligations are met. Codec patent exposure is separate from copyright licensing and depends on product/distribution circumstances. [mpv copyright](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mpv-player/mpv/master/Copyright), [FFmpeg legal and distribution checklist](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html).

The inspected UnRAR source license requires preserving specified notice text for modified redistribution and prohibits using it to recreate the proprietary compression algorithm; it is not a blanket GPL grant. Windows packaging copies this notice and several other notices, but the inspected script does not itself establish a complete libmpv/FFmpeg source-compliance record. Review potential license compatibility with the final combination; do not assume incompatibility or compatibility without that analysis. Local evidence: `vendor/unrar-sys/vendor/unrar/license.txt`, `tools/windows/package.ps1`, `assets/licenses/`, and vendored media-kit README/LICENSE files.

Separate application conduct information from software copyright permissions. Do not impose a universal personal-use-only, no-copying, no-modification, no-reverse-engineering or no-redistribution rule over components whose licenses grant those rights. If GPL applies, commercial use cannot be prohibited as an added GPL restriction. Describe the current project as intended for personal use without purporting to revoke applicable license rights. [GNU license FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html).

Inventory artwork, fonts, logos and screenshots separately. Do not claim ownership of third-party media or imply provider endorsement. TMDB's FAQ requires approved attribution and states both non-commercial API conditions and a prohibition on using its data/images in connection with pornographic content. BYO credentials do not waive this. Keep mainstream and specialist content paths distinguishable and obtain provider clarification before any proposed cross-enrichment. A future paid app, revenue feature or other commercial arrangement requires renewed provider-license review. [TMDB FAQ](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq).

## Consumer terms and reliability claims

Gemini eligibility also needs a specific product-fit decision. Its terms,
effective 23 March 2026, require users to be adults, restrict clients directed
toward or likely accessed by under-18s, specify professional/business rather
than consumer use, and require Paid Services for API clients made available in
the EEA, Switzerland or UK. A technically working free tier or user-supplied key
does not resolve whether this personal film-chat integration fits those terms.
Obtain provider clarification where necessary before promoting it as a free
consumer option. This finding does not silently disable existing user settings.
[Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

The Australian Consumer Law question depends on whether the actual supply and representations occur in trade or commerce and on the relevant provisions; neither a zero price nor a personal-project label alone resolves every issue. Do not assert either automatic exemption or that every consumer guarantee applies to this exact free release. Where guarantees do apply, they cannot be removed by a no-refunds statement or total denial of responsibility. Preserve non-excludable rights explicitly; do not use a liability cap of the zero purchase price as a supposed universal answer. [ACCC consumer guarantees](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees).

Unfair standard-form contract terms can attract penalties under reforms effective 9 November 2023. Avoid one-sided indemnities covering the maintainer's own wrongdoing, arbitrary termination of rights, unilateral retroactive changes and disproportionate exclusion clauses. Use clear effective dates and meaningful notice for material changes. Do not claim acknowledgment of a notice conclusively establishes enforceable contractual assent; counsel must decide whether any click-through agreement is necessary. [ACCC contracts guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/contracts).

Explain practical limits precisely: unsupported archive variants, recovery uncertainty, temporary disk use, data charges, provider retention/quota, source removal and unverified platform/device claims. State no uptime promise for independent services. Do not imply Zanzibarr takes provider subscription payments or controls provider refunds. There is no reason to copy NZBgeek's fabricated-payment-detail instruction, membership purge or IP restrictions.

## Copyright, downloads, casting and complaints

The current Copyright Act compilation reviewed is C2026C00138, effective 2 April 2026. Copyright authorization and infringement analysis turns on actual acts, control and reasonable measures; a lawful-use clause is useful information, not immunity. Personal/non-commercial use, payment for an indexer/provider, temporary buffering or finding a work on Usenet does not by itself establish permission. Assess download and casting use against actual rights; avoid blanket bans on lawful use as well. Technological-protection-measure rules have specific exceptions: a general player notice must not promise a general right to bypass DRM or source access controls. [Copyright Act 1968, current compilation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1968A00063/latest/text).

Publish a rights/abuse/security contact that the operator actually monitors. Request identification of the affected app/repository asset or controlled service, the claimant's authority, relevant work/location and contact details; do not solicit copyrighted media or identity documents in public GitHub issues. For an upstream indexer result, explain which provider controls the result and refer appropriately. Do not promise to erase Usenet content, remotely delete users' local files or close provider accounts. Separate intellectual-property complaints from urgent child-safety or non-consensual-image reports, without encouraging users to download examples.

Do not label the notice a certified DMCA process or assert section 512 safe-harbor eligibility. US safe harbors have activity-specific conditions, potentially including registered designated agents, repeat-infringer policies and notice/counter-notice processes. A generic copied email form or a promised 48-hour response is insufficient. A pure client distributor and a hosted linking/storage service require different analysis. If hosting is introduced, reassess both US and Australian eligibility and implement operational obligations before claiming protection. [US Copyright Office section 512 guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/512/), [Australian copyright enforcement review background](https://consultations.ag.gov.au/rights-and-protections/copyright-enforcement-review/user_uploads/copyright-enforcement-review-issues-paper.pdf).

## Adult features: concrete distribution decisions

Australian age-restricted-material codes started for hosting/carriage/search on 27 December 2025 and remaining covered sectors on 9 March 2026. Staged search and app-distribution age provisions reached commencement on 27 June and 9 September 2026 respectively. eSafety says a simple declaration of being 18 is insufficient for regulated pornography services. Actual duties depend on the service category, role and risk; this research has not determined whether each local adapter, remote service or distribution activity falls within a particular code. Neither “local-first,” “non-commercial” nor BYO APIs establishes an exemption. Obtain scope advice before making an assurance of compliance; use privacy-preserving, proportionate age assurance where legally required rather than adding identity-document collection casually. [eSafety sector/date and age-check guidance](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/codes/faq-access-to-online-porn-and-other-adult-content).

Australian Classification states that it does not classify websites, social media or apps; films and games have separate classification regimes. Do not describe a local adult toggle as an official R18+/X18+ classification, or assume the app itself needs a Classification Board film/game certificate. Any distribution of film content and app-store ratings requires its own analysis. [What Australian Classification classifies](https://www.classification.gov.au/classification-ratings/what-we-classify).

The UK rules require highly effective age assurance for in-scope services permitting pornography, with Part 3 duties applying from 25 July 2025. Assess the UK-link and service definitions rather than assuming Australian domicile avoids them. EU intermediary/platform duties similarly depend on service type and EU offering; their age-verification initiatives do not mean every offline player must collect ID. In the US, the Supreme Court upheld the challenged Texas age-verification requirement on 27 June 2025; that is not blanket approval of every state's law or a determination that this software falls within them. Review actual destination rules before promoting an adult catalog internationally. [Ofcom letter to services](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/about-ofcom/public-correspondence/2025/letter-to-part-3-services-that-allow-pornography.pdf?v=395643), [European Commission DSA scope FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/digital-services-act-questions-and-answers), [EU age-verification approach](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-age-verification), [Supreme Court opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/23-1122_3e04.pdf).

App-store permission is distinct from legality. Apple's guidelines prohibit pornography and impose further UGC requirements; Google Play also prohibits pornographic apps/content, with defined exceptions that should not be assumed to cover a dedicated adult catalog. An age rating or hidden toggle does not guarantee approval. Reassess store-bound feature availability and disclosures; never conceal functionality from reviewers. Desktop sideloading removes neither statutory nor provider obligations. [Apple guidelines 1.1.4/1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Google Play inappropriate-content policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9878810?hl=en).

## Owner and counsel decisions before public adoption

1. Confirm the legal operator, public contact, domicile, intended audiences and distribution territories. “Treewizard” is branding, not evidence of a legal entity. Do not invent an ABN, address, representative, registered agent or governing court.
2. Resolve application redistribution rights and the native-component source/license inventory. Preserve separate license rights in any terms page.
3. Confirm which content integrations ship, where TMDB is used, how explicit imagery is exposed and which provider contracts permit those uses. Review adult-service scope and operational controls with qualified counsel.
4. Approve a verified privacy inventory, retention/deletion controls and a workable confidential complaint route. Never promise automatic erasure or total anonymity without proof.
5. Decide whether pages are information notices or a contractual agreement. Keep mandatory rights, proportionate risk wording, version history and accessible persistent access. Do not imply a reading acknowledgment is legal sign-off.
6. Before paid support, hosting, exports/sync, accounts, advertising, payments or broader store rollout, revisit consumer, privacy, platform and content obligations. Codec/export questions become specific release-provenance and destination checks; do not append speculative universal waivers. Security and accessibility must be implemented and tested, not merely assigned to users in terms.

Remaining uncertainty is explicit: this is a source-based issue assessment, not full jurisdictional due diligence, a trademark clearance, a complete dependency audit, a content legality determination or an enforceability opinion. Obtain a lawyer's review of the identified blockers and final operator-specific pages before describing them as finalized legal terms.
